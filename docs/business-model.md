# Business Model: Independent Customs Clearing & Freight Forwarding Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-3331`
- ISCO-08: `3331`
- Occupation: Clearing and Forwarding Agents
- Social impact: local-jobs, supply-chain-resilience

## Customer

- importers
- exporters
- freight companies

## Offer

- customs clearance
- duty calculation
- freight forwarding coordination

## Revenue

- per-shipment fee
- contract retainer

## Trust Controls

- no customs declaration above the client's registered duty-value ceiling without governor gate
- shipment manifest verification required before any declaration is filed
- clearance and duty records are auditable, not editable
